When we think of the AI boom, the immediate thought goes to the rise of Gen AI companies like Anthropic and OpenAI, or the meteoric rise of Nvidia. 

If we dig deeper, though, the entire digital intelligence economy is currently bottlenecked by physical constraints: thermal management systems that prevent server racks from literally melting, precision actuators, and advanced packaging substrates. These represent supply chain chokeholds that most people in finance or software simply don't have the engineering vocabulary to identify.

This piece maps those hidden bottlenecks using primary-source numbers from company filings, earnings calls, and the hard thermodynamic constraints that traditional valuation models completely gloss over.

Written for the engineer who wants to understand where capital is actually moving, and for the finance professional who wants to understand why thermodynamics dictates the market.

title: Engineering in a hypothetical world with Superheroes
date: 2026-08-15
tag: What If?
summary: A serious technical attempt to bridge the gap between comic book physics and structural reality to see if modern infrastructure could actually survive a superhero
---

Comic books routinely hand-wave the physical aftermath of superhuman conflicts, but structural engineering doesn't have that luxury. If a superhero capable of generating the kinetic energy equivalent to a tactical missile strike actually operated within a metropolitan grid, the secondary effects would completely reshape building methodology and international insurance underwriting.

No really though, imagine it. If Spider-Man or Thor were in your neighborhood, crashing into buildings and latching onto concrete pillars while your car is hurled at them, you wouldn't be looking at the same architecture methods or insurance policies we have today.

Insurance companies wouldn’t just change their policies; they’d hire three aging Bollywood megastars to stand in slow-motion amidst flying red powder, doing a dramatic, two-finger salute to launch a luxury 'Premium Protection' tier, only for a legendary Indian cricketer to pop up on screen immediately after, pointing a bat at you and calmly saying, "Superhero Ka Khatra? Policy Sahi Hai."

A brilliant ad campaign, sure. But once the television turns off, you're still left with the actual engineering nightmare: how do you calculate premiums for a city where structural physics can be shattered on a casual Tuesday afternoon?

This project is a mathematically grounded attempt to calculate how modern civil infrastructure would react to that reality. We'll look at the exact stresses exerted on a high-rise foundation during an unassisted takeoff, structural load distribution, and the technicalities of insuring a city where structural physics can be broken at will.
